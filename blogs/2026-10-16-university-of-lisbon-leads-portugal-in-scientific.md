The University of Lisbon (ULisboa) ranks first in Portugal on Global Research Partnerships’ 2026 Industry Collaboration Index (ICI). Globally, it ranks 275th among 1,038 institutions, and in Europe, it ranks 119th among 388 universities evaluated. The index analyzes collaboration between universities and companies based on joint scientific output and documented research relationships between academic institutions and business groups.
