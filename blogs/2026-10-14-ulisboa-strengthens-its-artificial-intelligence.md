By the end of October 2026, students, faculty, researchers, and technical and administrative staff at the University of Lisbon will have access to the Education Plus edition of Google Workspace, which includes new features for artificial intelligence, productivity, collaboration, teaching, and security. This update is part of the suite of digital services provided by ULisboa and allows users to apply these tools in various contexts related to teaching, learning, research, and work.
